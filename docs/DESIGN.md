# PRIVATECRAFT Design System Specification

## Brand Identity & Aesthetic
PRIVATECRAFT is an ultra-luxury private aviation brand. The design architecture embraces modern dark mode with glassmorphic depth, refined typography, and gold accent highlights.

## Color Tokens
- **Background Obsidian (`--color-bg`)**: `#080A0D`
- **Surface Layer (`--color-surface`)**: `#121720`
- **Metallic Gold Accent (`--color-gold`)**: `#D4AF37`
- **Gold Glow (`--color-gold-glow`)**: `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`
- **Ivory Primary Text (`--color-ivory`)**: `#F5F5F0`
- **Cloud Muted Text (`--color-cloud`)**: `#94A3B8`

## Typography
- **Headings**: `Cormorant Garamond` (Serif, elegant high-contrast proportions)
- **Body & UI**: `Manrope` (Clean geometric sans-serif for legibility)

## Interactive Elements
- `.btn-gold`: Solid gold CTA button with subtle hover lift and glow
- `.btn-outline`: Glass border button with gold hover states
- `.card`: Dark glass card with smooth image scale transformations on hover
